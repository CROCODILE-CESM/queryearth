API reference

Everything below is importable from queryearth, except the shape helpers, which live in queryearth.space.

queryearth: match rules scoped by area, time and other dimensions to a request.

queryearth.time holds time keys, intervals and their measures, and queryearth.space points, polygons and closed-set measures (imported as from queryearth import space as sp). The rule engine is at the top level: an Index of dimensions, products, regions and rules answers queries with Finding objects, and emit() reports findings under a policy. See docs/design.md for the design.

class queryearth.ComputedRule[source]

A rule computed from the request or from data the caller holds.

Class keywords give its identity and scope: code, index (default Index.default()), abstract, the Outcome fields (kind defaults to caveat, severity to WARNING or INFO for a recommendation, title and message to the docstring), facets, axes, dims, space (a Space), time and requires (keys of Query.context). Defining a subclass registers one instance; a subclass inherits its bases’ keywords, merged, with its own winning (Q9).

check(query, match) runs only for a definite match and returns True (one finding with the declared outcome), False or None (none), a mapping of Finding fields to override, or several such mappings.

property outcome: Outcome

The declared outcome, before check() overrides it.

One kind of scope, declared on an Index under a unique name.

Optional members, looked up with getattr: comparable(rule, query) -> bool (False makes the finding conditional on the dimension); build(pairs) -> Prefilter over the (position, rule value) pairs of the rules that constrain it; to_json(overlap) for Finding.to_dict(); late = True to run after the exact spatial test; extent(rule) for what an undecided finding reports; always = True to call match(None, query) when a rule leaves it unconstrained.

query(query: Query | None = None, /, **fields: Any) → list[Finding][source]

Findings for one query with any number of requests, sorted.

suitable(query: Query, variable: str, *, products: Sequence[str] | None = None, score: Callable[[Product, Query, Sequence[Finding]], tuple[float, ...]] | None = None, include_dropped: bool = False) → list[Suitability][source]

The products that can serve a request, fewest known concerns first.

Not a quality ranking: empty findings mean only that no registered rule matched, and a product ranks higher simply because less has been written about it (see rule_counts()). Each candidate is judged on one request added to the query (section 10.1); one entirely outside its coverage is dropped, or listed last with include_dropped=True.

query_points(lon: Any, lat: Any, weights: Any = None, **fields: Any) → PointResult[source]

Masks and weighted fractions over many points (section 9.3).

fields are those of query() but space, context and Request.support: the points are every request’s support. Weights default to equal, and zero weights leave points out of every fraction. NaN points are inside no rule. A computed rule takes part through its mask(lon, lat, query) hook, called with the flat arrays, and is otherwise listed in skipped.

queryearth.default_score(product: Product, query: Query, findings: Sequence[Finding]) → tuple[float, float, float][source]

(worst, burden, misfit): lower means fewer known concerns (section 10.2).

Counts definite caveat and coverage findings not below their min_overlap; burden weighs severities 1, 2, 4 by area and time fractions (1.0 if None).

queryearth.emit(findings: Iterable[Finding], mode: Any = None, **changes: Any) → list[Finding][source]

Report findings under the active policy; returns those logged, warned or sunk.

mode and changes override the policy for this call. Dropped: conditional findings, caveats and coverage below min_severity, area_fraction below min_overlap, codes or prefixes in ignore, drafts unless include_drafts, recommendations unless show_recommendations, and repeats of (code, query_label, product). Recommendations are logged at INFO or sunk, never warned or raised; in “raise” mode, or at or above raise_at, caveats and coverage findings are raised together after the rest are reported.

queryearth.interval(start: Any, end: Any) → TimeSpec[source]

A closed interval whose ends are inclusive at their precision.

Ends are ISO 8601 strings, “..” or None for an open end, or date-like values. A date covers its whole day, and a value with a time of day covers its second, even at midnight.

queryearth.parse_time(member: Mapping[str, Any] | None, **props: Any) → TimeSpec | None[source]

Read a JSON-FG time member with the record’s time properties.

props may hold every record property; periods, recurrence, time_start and time_end are read and the rest ignored. Returns None when the record has no time and no recurrence, which means always; an interval open at both ends, with no periods or recurrence, also means always.

queryearth.space

Space: points and polygons with straight lon/lat edges, and closed-set measures.

Coordinates are float64 (longitude, latitude) in degrees on WGS84 (CRS84), and edges are straight lines in longitude-latitude. Every polygon is normalised once, when it is built: longitudes wrapped exactly, rings cut at +-180, invalid rings repaired and rings oriented; a ring that goes around a pole is refused (design.md section 6). Areas use a closed form on a sphere of radius R_KM.

Import as from queryearth import space as sp.

class queryearth.space.Space(kind: Literal['point', 'polygon'], geometry: Geometry, area_km2: float | None = None)[source]

A point or a normalised (multi)polygon; compares and hashes by identity.

geometry is a shapely Point or (Multi)Polygon in [-180, 180] with no part crossing +-180; area_km2 is the spherical area of a polygon, None for a point.

queryearth.space.area_km2(geometry: Geometry) → float[source]

Spherical area of the polygon parts of a geometry, holes subtracted.

queryearth.space.box(west: float, south: float, east: float, north: float) → Space[source]

A box from west to east between two latitudes.

west > east crosses the antimeridian and gives two parts, and -180 to 180 is a full latitude band, for a domain periodic in longitude.

queryearth.space.measure(rule: Space | Geometry | None, query: Space) → tuple[float | None, float | None][source]

(area_fraction, overlap_km2) of a query space against a rule shape.

area_fraction is 1.0 or 0.0 for a point, and for a polygon the spherical area of the intersection over the query’s area. overlap_km2 is None for a point. A global rule (None) covers all; an empty query has no measures.

queryearth.space.meets(rule: Space | Geometry | None, query: Space) → bool[source]

Whether a query space meets a normalised rule shape, as closed sets.

rule is a polygon Space or shapely geometry, or None for a global rule. A point on an edge meets the shape, shapes that touch across +-180 meet there, and an empty query meets nothing.

queryearth.space.nowhere() → Space[source]

An empty polygon, for a callable coverage that does not serve a query.

queryearth.space.point(lon: float, lat: float) → Space[source]

A point, such as a station or a grid cell centre.

queryearth.space.points_mask(rule: Space | Geometry | None, lon: Any, lat: Any) → ndarray[source]

Closed-set membership of points in a rule shape, shaped like lon.

The points inside the shape’s bounding box are tested with prepared intersects_xy, never contains_xy, so points on an edge are inside; points at +-180 also test the other side of the seam. Points with a NaN coordinate are inside no rule.

queryearth.space.polygon(shape: Any) → Space[source]

A normalised polygon from shapely, GeoJSON or a list of (lon, lat) positions.

Longitudes may use any convention; holes are normalised as separate rings and subtracted. A ring that goes around a pole raises ValueError.

queryearth.space.wrap_lon(x: Any) → Any[source]

x in [-180, 180] with no rounding error, as a float or an array like x.

180 and -180 stay as given. Adding or subtracting 360 is exact, since the remainder and 360 are within a factor of two of each other.
